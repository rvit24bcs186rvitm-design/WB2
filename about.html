About CAREER CONNECT

Our Purpose

CAREER CONNECT is a demonstration internship application portal built for a Web Technology laboratory assignment. It helps college students explore internship roles and submit applications through a clean, modern interface.

How It Works

  1. Browse available internships on the Internships page.
  2. Click Apply Now on your preferred role.
  3. Fill in personal, educational, and internship details.
  4. Upload your resume (PDF, max 2 MB).
  5. Submit and view your application reference number.

Important Notice

This is an academic demonstration project. It is not a real recruitment service, and applications do not reach any real companies. No personal data or resumes are stored permanently.
